Before the shortlist grows

Make three checks that matter.

A clear question is more useful than another saved thumbnail.

01 / IDENTITY

Is it the same item?

Start with original seller link verification. Match the item identifier, then make a clothing variant comparison: a shared cover image does not tell you which color, fabric or size is selected.

02 / FIT

What will you measure?

For a sneaker size chart comparison, check the model and the chart's units. For apparel, use a similar garment you own as a reference and keep the measuring method consistent.

03 / EVIDENCE

What is still unknown?

Write one unresolved question beside each candidate. An accessory dimensions comparison, a fabric label photo or a clearer view of the stitching may resolve it more effectively than a general description.

Research / source details

MyCNBox finds: shortlist the item, not the thumbnail

Start a shortlist with one use in mind: an everyday layer, a particular silhouette or an accessory of a certain size. Save the original listing and item identifier alongside the selected option. A screenshot can preserve context, but it should not replace the source link.

When two candidates look alike, compare the information that distinguishes them: measurements, fabric description, closure details and available photographs. Record missing information as an open question. If a link stops working, search using the saved identifier and verify any replacement rather than assuming the first similar image is the same item.

Fit / measurement habits

MyCNBox sizing guide: start with something that fits

Lay out a similar garment you already wear comfortably. Measure it using the same points shown in the seller's diagram. Record centimeters or inches explicitly, and distinguish garment dimensions from the body measurements a chart may recommend.

For relaxed clothing, compare width and length separately; choosing a larger label can change both. For footwear, keep your foot measurement, the listing's model-specific guidance and your preferred fit together. If a chart is ambiguous, ask for clarification before deciding. A size label alone is too little information for a reliable comparison.

QC / visible details

MyCNBox QC guide: ask a question the photo can answer

A clothing QC photo checklist should focus on the selected color and size, print position, seams and visible marks. For a sneaker stitching photo review, request comparable views of both shoes. If fit is the concern, warehouse measurement photos should show the tape's starting point and the full span being measured.

Use a close-up hardware inspection for clasps, zips or buckles that are unclear in a wider shot. To compare product color under neutral lighting, request another image if shadows or exposure obscure it. Photos help you review what is visible; they do not prove authenticity, long-term durability or comfort.

Planning / parcel notes

MyCNBox shipping checklist: separate the cost layers

Keep item and domestic shipping costs separate from any applicable service charges and international freight. An international parcel weight estimate is a planning note, not a final quote. Record packed parcel dimensions when available and check how the chosen route calculates chargeable weight.

Review shipping route restrictions for the destination and item type before paying. Confirm delivery tracking expectations, included services and any applicable destination charges with the provider. Compare options using the same parcel details; a lower headline amount may describe a different service. Keep the latest confirmed quote beside the date you checked it.

Straight answers

Questions worth asking first.

Use these answers to understand the page and decide what information you still need.

What is a MyCNBox spreadsheet?

It is a product research list associated with searching for MyCNBox shopping references. It may be a sheet or a web directory. Use it to organize seller links and comparison notes, then confirm the live listing before ordering.

Is this an official MyCNBox website?

No. Fieldnotes is an independent editorial page. Its category shortcuts open CAFANS categories, and its other navigation links open the CAFANS homepage.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No spreadsheet download is hosted here. This page provides category shortcuts and original buying guides. You can keep your own comparison sheet using the fields described in the articles.

Can QC photos prove authenticity or guarantee fit?

No. Photos can help you inspect visible details and measurements, but they do not establish authenticity, comfort, durability or a guaranteed fit.

How should I compare sizes across listings?

Measure a similar item you already own, use the same units and measurement method, and compare with the selected listing's size chart. Check whether the chart gives garment measurements or body measurements.

Is the listed item price the full delivered cost?

Do not assume so. Record the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service charges and international freight separately. Check any destination charges and confirm the current total before paying.
